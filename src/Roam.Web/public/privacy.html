Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Roam is run by OPERATOR_NAME ("we"). This page explains what information Roam collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile: display name, country and date of birth. Your date of birth is used only to confirm that you are 18 or older.
  • Location: the places you ask to see, and, if you volunteer, the approximate location you choose to share so nearby requests can reach you.
  • Tours: your tour requests, the tours you host or join, in-tour chat messages, feedback and ratings, safety reports and blocks.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request logs, used to keep the service secure (for example, rate limiting and abuse prevention).

Video and audio

Live video and audio travel directly between the people in a tour (or through a relay server when a direct connection is not possible). Roam does not record or store video or audio. Your camera and microphone are only used after you allow them in your browser.

How we use it

  • To run the service: match tour requests with volunteers, connect tours and show chat.
  • To keep people safe: verify that users are adults, review reports and act on abuse.
  • To send account emails, such as email confirmation and password-reset codes.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The other participants of a tour see your display name and the chat you send in that tour.
  • Service providers that host or deliver parts of Roam (for example hosting, email delivery, the video relay service and map tiles) process data only to provide that service.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, location and settings are removed, your chat messages are replaced with a placeholder, and your open requests are cancelled. Some records (such as safety reports and moderation decisions) may be kept where needed to protect other users.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Profile → "Download my data".
  • Delete your account: Profile → "Delete my account".
  • You can also contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL to ask about, correct or delete your data.

Children

Roam is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow anyone younger to use it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
